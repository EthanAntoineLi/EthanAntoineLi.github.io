02 / Mission

Two machines.
One job.

I ski. Every winter the same shape of accident: a person down on a line that is difficult, exposed, or both — and a team that has to commit people to that ground.

The grand plan is one full robot that does the rescue job itself. Drones work further out and alongside it — looking around, watching for dangers, helping it locate the person. The robot will have its own visual detection; the drone is its larger eye, more of the environment from further away.

The two machines do not work together yet.
